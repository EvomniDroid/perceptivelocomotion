Policy interface
----------------
Actor input: 360 float32 values (8 frames x 45 values)
Per-frame order:
  joint_pos[12], joint_vel[12], base_ang_vel[3], projected_gravity[3],
  velocity_command[vx, vy, wz], previous_raw_action[12]
Actor output: 12 raw leg joint-position residual actions
Joint order: FL/FR/RL/RR hip, FL/FR/RL/RR thigh, FL/FR/RL/RR calf
Action scale: 0.3 rad around the target2/default joint pose
Action bounds: [-1, 1]
Action low-pass: filtered = 0.8 * raw + 0.2 * previous_filtered
The actor has no foot-contact, base-linear-velocity, gait-phase, or arm output.

Finetune changes
----------------
Swing-foot clearance is rewarded only while a foot is airborne, relative to
local terrain height. The target clearance band is 0.04-0.08 m.
An aperiodic diagonal-contact reward encourages diagonal support without adding
a gait clock or gait-phase observation. Foot-slide penalty was strengthened and
leg joint-deviation regularization was relaxed.
