B2RM velocity real-deploy training
===================================

Shared policy interface
-----------------------
- All stages use the historical 72-D observation and 18-D action layout.
- Policy leg PD: Kp=250, Kd=5.
- Leg action scale: 0.4; raw policy action clip: [-1.5, 1.5].
- Arm action scale: 0.0; the arm stays in the folded carry pose.
- Policy control: 50 Hz; simulation physics: 200 Hz.
- Training terrain: the low-amplitude flat-training terrain set.
- Startup push and reset force disturbances are disabled.

Stage 0: policy stand pretraining
---------------------------------
Task:
  Instinct-B2RM-Velocity-Stand-v0

Flow on every reset:
  target2 standing pose at 0.50 m
  -> Kp=250, Kd=5 from the first simulation step
  -> policy controls immediately with a zero velocity command
  -> rewards are active immediately
  -> hard fall termination gets 0.5 s of initial settling grace

Train from scratch before training the handoff:

  cd ~/isaac/instinctlab
  python scripts/instinct_rl/train.py \
    --task=Instinct-B2RM-Velocity-Stand-v0 \
    --headless \
    --num_envs=2048 \
    --max_iterations=5000

Stage 1: instantaneous stand takeover
-------------------------------------
Task:
  Instinct-B2RM-Velocity-Handoff-Stand-v0

Flow on every reset:
  prone
  -> 0.8 s target1 interpolation at Kp=1000, Kd=10
  -> 0.8 s target2 interpolation at Kp=1000, Kd=10
  -> 0.4 s target2 hold at Kp=1000, Kd=10
  -> instantaneous gain switch to Kp=250, Kd=5
  -> policy takes full control on the same frame (no action blend)
  -> rear calf contact above 50 N for 0.30 s resets the episode
  -> zero-command policy stand

Warm-start from the accepted Stage-0 checkpoint:

  cd ~/isaac/instinctlab
  python scripts/instinct_rl/train.py \
    --task=Instinct-B2RM-Velocity-Handoff-Stand-v0 \
    --headless \
    --num_envs=2048 \
    --max_iterations=10000 \
    --resume \
    --load_run=/absolute/path/to/stage0_run \
    --checkpoint=model_5000.pt

Acceptance check:
- Remains upright for at least 30 seconds after takeover.
- No isolated leg kick during the 1000 -> 250 gain switch.
- Raw actions do not continuously hit [-1.5, 1.5].
- Base velocity remains close to zero.

Stage 2: forward walking
------------------------
Task:
  Instinct-B2RM-Velocity-Handoff-Walk-v0

Flow on every reset:
  historical converged velocity-baseline reset and PPO flow
  -> target2 standing pose at 0.50 m, Kp=250, Kd=5
  -> leg action scale 0.4 (same as commit b8329be)
  -> forward command sampled uniformly from 0.0 to 0.5 m/s
  -> no handoff action blend, termination grace, or gated calf reset

Train this baseline from scratch first. Its accepted checkpoint can later
warm-start the stand-handoff fine-tuning task because their policy observation
and action layouts are identical:

  cd ~/isaac/instinctlab
  python scripts/instinct_rl/train.py \
    --task=Instinct-B2RM-Velocity-Handoff-Walk-v0 \
    --headless \
    --num_envs=2048 \
    --max_iterations=5000

Do not add yaw, lateral velocity, rough terrain, pushes, payload
randomization, or PD randomization until this flat forward policy passes
Isaac Play and real-robot low-speed validation.
